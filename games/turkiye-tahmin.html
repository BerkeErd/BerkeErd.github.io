About The Game

A free browser-based statistics guessing game built around real data from Türkiye. Its 34,000+ question pool is generated from current datasets covering population, names, migration, education, geography, and the economy, with daily challenges, mixed rounds, an endless mode, and Türkiyedle.

Real Data, Fresh Questions

Questions are generated from datasets published by TÜİK, Wikidata, the World Bank, and Wikimedia, turning public statistics into short, replayable guessing rounds.

Four Ways to Play

Günün Tahmini gives everyone the same ten questions, Karışık Oyna starts a fresh mixed round, Sonsuz Mod adds a three-life challenge, and Türkiyedle offers a daily name or city puzzle.
